🔧 Setup & Installation

❓ How do I enable the UnderText keyboard?
  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  2. Go to General → Keyboard → Keyboards
  3. Tap "Add New Keyboard..."
  4. Select "UnderText" from the list
  5. When typing, press and hold the 🌐 globe icon to switch to UnderText
❓ Why does UnderText need "Full Access"?

Full Access permission is required for:

  • AI Translation: Network access for Azure OpenAI (Premium only)
  • On-Device Translation: Apple Translation framework (works offline, iOS 26.0+)
  • Word Pronunciation: On-device speech synthesis
  • Dictionary Lookup: Word definition API access
Privacy Guarantee: UnderText does NOT track, log, or collect your keystrokes. We do not have servers or analytics. Your data stays on your device.
❓ What iOS version do I need?

UnderText requires iOS 26.0 or later to use the latest Apple Translation API.

This ensures the best translation quality and privacy with on-device processing.

⌨️ Keyboard Features

❓ What keyboard layouts are available?

UnderText includes 6 keyboard layouts:

  • QWERTY - Standard English/International
  • QWERTZ - German (with ü, ö, ä)
  • AZERTY - French (with context-sensitive accents)
  • Spanish QWERTY - Spanish (with ñ)
  • Korean Dubeolsik - Korean (두벌식 2-set)
  • Vietnamese Telex - Vietnamese with Telex input

Enable "Auto-adjust Layout" in settings to automatically switch based on your input language.

❓ How does Vietnamese Telex input work?

Telex is a typing method for Vietnamese characters:

  • w alone → ư
  • aw → ă
  • ow → ơ
  • aa → â
  • ee → ê
  • oo → ô
  • dd → đ

Tone marks:

  • a + s → á (sắc)
  • a + f → à (huyền)
  • a + r → ả (hỏi)
  • a + x → ã (ngã)
  • a + j → ạ (nặng)

Auto-correction: ưo → ươ for valid syllables (e.g., "lươn", "tươi")

🐛 Troubleshooting

❓ Translation isn't working. What should I check?
  1. Full Access: Make sure it's enabled in iOS Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → UnderText
  2. Internet: Azure OpenAI (Premium) requires internet (Apple Translation works offline)
  3. Language packs: For Apple Translation, download language packs in the Translate app
  4. Premium tokens: If you're Premium and ran out of AI tokens, the app falls back to unlimited Apple Translation
  5. iOS version: Ensure you have iOS 26.0 or later
❓ The keyboard isn't showing up
  1. Verify UnderText is added: Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards
  2. Restart your iPhone
  3. Try switching keyboards by pressing and holding the 🌐 globe icon
  4. Remove and re-add the keyboard if issues persist
❓ How do I reset my settings?

In the main app:

  1. Scroll to the bottom
  2. Tap "Reset Settings" in the About section
  3. Confirm

This resets all preferences to defaults but keeps your flashcards and subscription.

📚 Learning Features

❓ How do flashcards work?

Save vocabulary for later review:

  1. After translating, tap any word in the translation
  2. Select "Learn" (⭐ icon)
  3. Word pair is saved to your flashcard deck
  4. Review in main app → Flashcards tab

The spaced repetition system automatically schedules reviews for optimal retention.
